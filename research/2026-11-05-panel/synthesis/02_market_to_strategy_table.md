# Market-to-Strategy Table (shortlisted conditions)

**Status key:** OBSERVED = inspected in this workspace (nothing qualifies — the workspace is empty); EXT = externally supported mechanism (public); HYP = hypothesis; RC = **research candidate** (evidence insufficient to rank slots). Slots are MECHANISM CLASSES because no strategy definitions (KX, PX, engulfing, weak reversal, sell-off recovery) exist in the workspace; the user maps each real slot to a class by its entry trigger, stop and invalidation — **similar names do not establish similar exposures**.

Mechanism classes: **BO** breakout/continuation · **FB** failed-break reversal · **MR** mean-reversion to (event-)VWAP · **PR** post-release momentum.
