# J3 — Head trader / product owner verdict

Lens: does the input exist on a retail feed at bar close, is it there every day, can the trader read it in 10 s, can the action be sent in that window. Judged on content; agreement earned nothing. Standing condition: nothing reaches the live screen until the eligible-signal log exists and the slot clears the max-of-N check under conservative fills.

## Prototype

In 10 s it shows headline, state pills, exposure delta and "what changes this". The states differ in wording and denominator, not in shape or control: same pill shape, account limit coloured blue (reads as information, not a lock), no buttons. Wrong or missing:

1. ACCOUNT LIMIT REACHED never renders in any scenario. In scenario 4 both slots become INPUT UNAVAILABLE before the account gate runs, and the open MNQ short at −$62 unrealized against −$40 daily headroom is already a breach under a rule that counts open P&L; the screen should be LOCKED, not "NO NEW TRADE".
2. Scenario 3 says "TAKE (baseline size)" while order and risk are computed at size 2 — the untested calm-sizing hypothesis executes live.
3. Every input is required by every slot (`some(v!=='ok')`); a stale ES print kills sell-off-recovery, which may not read ES. Slots must declare required inputs.
4. Opposite-direction signals against an open position (scenario 4: LONG signals, open SHORT) are unhandled; a reversal doubles cost and risk.
5. Headline lacks side/qty/instrument/price. No signal age, no "valid until price X", no release countdown in the header, no engine heartbeat — NOT FIRED and "engine dead" look identical.
6. "R-ticks" is unreadable at speed; show net $/trade with n/sessions.

Main screen: time, release countdown, action line (side, qty, instrument, entry/stop/target), state word with n/sessions and $/trade, exposure before/after, headroom including unrealized, signal age, keys. Drill-down: CI, condition cells, Study 66/71 material, slippage history, provenance, "why did it change".

## What the director did not ask

Whether the 10-second human loop should exist per slot. Between t_signal and fill the entry drifts; a hand-confirmed order on a slot with an 8-point edge loses a measurable share to that drift, and nobody was asked to measure it. Decision changed: which slots auto-route with a server-side bracket and which keep confirmation. Test: from the eligible-signal log over 30 sessions, median (fill − intended) and t_fill − t_signal by slot; auto-route any slot where median drift exceeds 25% of its net edge, keep confirmation where it is under 10%. The schema already carries the fields; the test is cheap and changes a daily action.
